# Proactive Safety Agent: weekly safety health report (mock-up)

A clickable mock-up of the weekly safety health report produced by the **Proactive Safety Agent** (Agent 04) for VZI. Zensar is building the agent.

**This page uses randomly generated example data. It contains no real Enablon records.**

## Open it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser; it is one self-contained file. Fonts load from Google Fonts when online.

## What it shows

- **Verdict:** the overall grade (A to E) for VZI, BMM and Gamsberg, with the AI verdict and its business impact.
- **AI summary:** the actions for this week, with what each can cause, the owner and a link to the details. Choose "AI summary" at the top to read only this.
- **Vital signs (report card):** field presence, hazard exposure, critical control checks, action follow-through and hazard close-out, each compared with its usual range.
- **Focus areas:** up to four, each with what it can cause and what to do.
- **Major Unwanted Events in levels:** 3 domains (L1), 12 groups (L2) and 34 MUEs (L3), from a draft taxonomy for VZI HSE review. The page opens at the groups; select one to see its MUEs.
- **Where the problem is,** what went well, and how the report is built.
- **One email per site** (BMM and Gamsberg).

## Menus

- **Layout:** 10 page structures (briefing, single-screen dashboard, tabs, issue list and detail, scorecard table, MUE risk register, briefing note, heat map first, step-through, sites side by side).
- **Design:** 10 sober design languages for review.
- **Data:** this week, or three example scenarios (a good week, a pit wall warning, a reporting drop).

## Notes

- Text marked "AI insight" is the agent's analysis. The HSE Head confirms the focus areas.
- Grade bands and ranking weights are proposals to agree with VZI HSE.
- Leading indicators only; no predictions.
